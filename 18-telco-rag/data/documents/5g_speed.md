# 5G Speed

## Marketing Claims

5G is much faster than previous generations, delivering download speeds of up to 1 Gbps (gigabits per second) in ideal conditions.

An example: file download times drop dramatically compared to 4G — a full HD movie can be downloaded in under a minute.

## Benefits

- Up to 10 Gbps peak speeds in the most favorable deployments
- Near-instant downloads for streaming and cloud applications
- Low latency combined with high throughput for enhanced mobile broadband

## Ideal Conditions

The highest speeds require strong line-of-sight to the serving tower, minimal network load, and early experimental spectrum bands.
