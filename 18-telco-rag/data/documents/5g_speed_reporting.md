# 5G Speed Reporting

## What Customers Experience

In real deployments there is no single 5G speed. Customers typically see download speeds between 100 and 300 Mbps depending on their location.

## Variability Drivers

- Distance from the cell site and building materials
- Number of active users in the area at that moment
- Network configuration, spectrum band, and device capabilities

## KPIs and Reporting

Network engineers report speed performance per cell site using median and P95 throughput figures rather than a headline "up to" number.

Operator SLAs quote a minimum data throughput guarantee, not a marketing maximum, so reported figures vary by site and time of day.
