# SIM Activation

## Symptoms
- SIM not recognized by network
- "No SIM" error displayed
- Unable to make calls or send SMS

## Common Causes
- SIM card not properly inserted
- SIM card not activated by carrier
- Incompatible SIM size
- Carrier activation pending

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Reinsert SIM card ensuring proper contact
2. Check SIM card is the correct size for the device
3. Contact carrier to verify SIM activation status
4. Test SIM in another device to isolate the issue
5. Replace SIM card if damaged

## Prevention
- Handle SIM cards with care
- Ensure correct SIM size before insertion
- Keep carrier contact information handy
