# Network Escalation

## Symptoms
- Issue cannot be resolved at L1 support level
- Problem requires higher-level expertise
- Affects multiple users or critical services

## Escalation Tiers
- L1: Basic troubleshooting, checklists
- L2: Advanced troubleshooting, configuration review
- L3: Engineering analysis, root cause identification
- L4: Vendor engagement, external support

## Escalation Criteria
- Issue unresolved after 2 hours at L1
- Affects critical business services
- Requires vendor-specific expertise
- Security implications detected

## Handoff Process
1. Comprehensive summary of issue and steps taken
2. Relevant logs and monitoring data
3. Impact assessment and business context
4. Desired outcome and timeline expectations
5. All documentation transferred securely
